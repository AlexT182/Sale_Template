**[TIẾNG ANH]**

- **Product Name:** Weasel Coffee
- **Features:** Fermented in the laboratory, strong taste, very good digestive support --> Beautiful skin
- **Packaging (Inner/Outer):** 200-300 grams in a luxury box + Filter or/and Cup, hand blender / 10-12 boxes/carton
